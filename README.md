# E-Commerce Customer & Transaction Analytics

## 📊 Project Overview

An end-to-end E-Commerce Customer & Transaction Analytics project built using MySQL, Python, Pandas, and Power BI.

The project analyzes customer behavior, transactions, products, revenue trends, order performance, and customer segmentation to generate meaningful business insights.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- MySQL
- Python
- Pandas
- Power BI
- DAX
- Data Visualization

## 🎯 Business Questions

- What is the overall revenue and order performance?
- How does revenue change over time?
- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- Which products are the top revenue contributors?
- Which cities generate the highest revenue?
- What is the distribution of order statuses?
- How are customers distributed across RFM segments?

## 💡 Key Insights

- Generated ₹213.33M in total revenue from 50,000 orders.
- Sports generated the highest category revenue at approximately ₹40.81M.
- UPI was the most frequently used payment method, accounting for 37.87% of orders.
- 86.88% of orders were delivered, while 8.27% were cancelled and 4.84% were returned.
- Chennai generated the highest city-level revenue at approximately ₹22.99M.
- RFM segmentation identified customer groups including High Value, Recent Customers, At Risk, Loyal & Recent, and High Value At Risk.
